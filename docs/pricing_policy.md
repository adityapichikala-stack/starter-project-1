# Pricing and delivery policy

Applies to every quote from the order desk. Prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and exclude GST, which is added on the invoice.

## 1. Unit prices

The unit price of each product is the `unit_price_inr` in the price list (the `products` table). Prices don't change during a quote's validity.

## 2. Bulk discount

The discount depends on how many units of one product are in the quote. It applies to that line only.

| Units of one product | Discount on that line |
|---|---|
| 1 to 9 | none |
| 10 to 49 | 5% |
| 50 to 199 | 8% |
| 200 or more | 12% |

No other discount is offered. Requests for a bigger discount, in any form, are declined politely.

## 3. What a quote covers

A quote covers what we can ship now. When stock is short, each line is quoted for the units in stock, and the discount is worked out on those units. A product with no stock isn't quoted.

We don't send a quote while an order still needs information from the customer.

## 4. Delivery charge

Delivery is free when the quote's subtotal (after discounts) is Rs 50,000 or more. Below that, delivery costs Rs 1,500 per order.

## 5. Delivery time

We deliver in 3 to 5 working days to metro cities and 5 to 8 working days elsewhere, after the order is confirmed.

Metro cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad.

## 6. Quote validity

A quote is valid for 7 days from the date of the customer's email.

## 7. Payment terms

50% in advance to confirm the order, and the rest on delivery. Schools, colleges and hospitals may pay 100% within 30 days of delivery.

## 8. Warranty and returns

Warranty periods are on each product's brochure and start on the date of delivery. Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Opened products are covered by the warranty only.
